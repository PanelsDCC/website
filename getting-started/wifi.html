Configure Wi‑Fi

Only ever use your layout at home? If Wi‑Fi was already set in Raspberry Pi Imager and your network has not changed, you probably do not need to do anything here — open Connect if you want to double-check, or go straight to Connecting your DCC controller/s. The steps below matter most for hotspot mode, travel, or extra saved networks.

Manage how your Raspberry Pi joins networks in PanelsDCC Connect — home Wi‑Fi, saved access points, and portable hotspot.

Step 1: Open Connect, then the Wi‑Fi tab

On a phone, laptop, or the Pi (same network as the Pi), open Connect below. In the Connect window, select the Wi‑Fi tab — that is where the rest of this page happens.

PanelsDCC Connect

Port 9000 — after it loads, use the Wi‑Fi tab (not Control).

Use the same hostname as in Raspberry Pi Imager (we recommend panels-dcc). You can also enter a .local name or IP address. Your choice is saved in this browser.

PanelsDCC Connect port 9000

Wi‑Fi, DCC hardware, controller connections.

http://panels-dcc:9000/

Open Connect

Step 2: Client mode or hotspot mode

  • Client mode: the Pi joins a Wi‑Fi network you’ve saved (for example your home layout network).
  • Hotspot mode: the Pi creates its own hotspot so phones and tablets can connect directly — handy at shows or when there is no trusted network.

When you leave the area of your saved networks, Connect can switch to hotspot automatically, or you can change mode yourself — most useful when you travel with the layout or operate away from your usual network.

Step 3: Hotspot QR code

In hotspot mode, Connect shows a QR code so camera-equipped devices can join quickly. Consider printing it and keeping it on or near the Pi case.

Step 4: Add or update known networks

From the Wi‑Fi tab, scan for nearby networks, pick one, and enter the password to save it as a known network for later sessions. Update this list when your venue or home network changes.
