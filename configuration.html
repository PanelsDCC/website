Configuration

Powerful control without complexity

One of the defining principles of Panels DCC is that everything is configured visually. There is no requirement to write scripts, edit configuration files, or understand complex command structures. If you can drag, drop, and click, you can build and operate a sophisticated DCC layout.

From trains and accessories to full signalling-style panels, configuration happens directly in the interface, with immediate visual feedback. This makes Panels DCC approachable for beginners, while still offering the depth needed for advanced layouts.

Before you create an account or subscription

Cloud-based design and your account work together with PanelsDCC Connect and PanelsDCC Control on your Raspberry Pi. You should have Connect and Control installed, working, and verified on your layout before you create a PanelsDCC account or take out a subscription.

That means: your command station is detected and controllable in Connect, and Control shows a healthy connection to Connect so you can drive at least one locomotive and one accessory as expected. If something is wrong locally, fixing it after you subscribe does not change the fact that it is your responsibility to ensure your local hardware, wiring, and network are ready before you commit to an account or paid plan.

Use these pages in order:

  1. Installation — install Connect and Control on the Pi.
  2. Connecting your DCC controller — attach and verify your command station in Connect.
  3. Driving your first train — open Control, check connection status, and confirm basic driving works.
  4. Configure trains, accessories & panels — design and sync in PanelsDCC (this page).

Only after that should you create an account and use cloud configuration for trains, accessories, and panels.

Train Setup Made Effortless

Load, configure, and drive — visually

Panels DCC draws on an extensive UK locomotive library, allowing new trains to be added simply by typing a description. Images and core details are loaded instantly, removing the need for manual data entry or hunting for reference material.

For sound-fitted locomotives, Panels DCC includes a growing library of DCC function maps. Instead of remembering function numbers, you control features through clear, recognisable icons — lights look like lights, horns look like horns.

Unlike many systems, Panels DCC also lets you:

  • Create your own custom functions and function maps
  • Upload and assign your own icons to functions and maps
  • Add functions to existing maps
  • Reorder functions so your favourites appear first

This means the control interface adapts to how you drive, not the other way around.

Accessories That Look Alive

Signals, points, and layout features with real presence

Accessory control in Panels DCC is just as visual as train control.

We provide a growing set of pre-built animated accessories, including:

  • Digital colour-light signals
  • Semaphore signals with realistic movement
  • Turnouts and other layout elements

These aren't abstract symbols — they are designed to look like the real thing, making the state of the layout immediately obvious during operation.

You can also define your own accessories, using non-animated visuals that change automatically based on state. This allows Panels DCC to support everything from bespoke scenic features to custom-built hardware.

Panels: Where It All Comes Together

One interface. One railway.

All of this — trains, functions, accessories, and visuals — comes together in Panels.

Panels are configured entirely visually, by dragging and dropping elements into place. Tracks, signals, points, and routes are arranged to match your real layout, creating a single, coherent control surface.

From a panel, you can:

  • Drive trains
  • Set routes
  • Operate signals and points
  • See the live state of the railway at a glance

Rather than switching between multiple screens or control styles, Panels DCC gives you one integrated interface for running your entire layout — whether you're shunting a yard, running a timetable, or operating with multiple people.

Built for Growth

Start simple. Go as deep as you want.

Panels DCC is designed to scale with your railway. You can begin with basic train control and manual accessories, then progressively introduce custom function map, routes and accessory macros, and full panel operation as your layout evolves.

Everything you configure becomes part of a shared visual language — making operation smoother, more realistic, and far more enjoyable.

Cloud-Based Configuration

Design from anywhere

Because configuration lives in the cloud, layouts can be designed, refined, and extended from anywhere — without needing to be physically connected to the railway.

This means you can:

  • Plan and configure your layout at home
  • Refine panel layouts on a tablet
  • Make adjustments from any device with a web browser
  • Share configurations across multiple devices

The same cloud-based approach that powers multi-device operation also makes configuration flexible and accessible.
