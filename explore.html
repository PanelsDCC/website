Operating Panels DCC

Panels DCC is a flexible control system for Digital Command Control layouts, designed to bring together train driving, accessory operation, and route-based panel control into a single, coherent experience.

Whether you want hands-on driving, realistic signal-box style operation, or a hybrid of both, Panels DCC adapts to how you want to run your railway.

Panels DCC adapts seamlessly across mobile phones, tablets, and desktops. Multiple clients can run simultaneously with live state synchronization — changes on one device appear instantly on all others.

Train Operation

Drive trains, not menus

Panels DCC provides intuitive control of locomotives while respecting how real railways are operated.

Each train can be controlled individually, with clear visibility of speed, direction, and functions. Controls are designed to be readable at a glance and usable during active operation — not buried behind complex menus.

Key capabilities include:

  • Independent control of multiple locomotives
  • Smooth speed and direction control
  • Access to decoder functions such as lights, sound, and auxiliary features
  • Clear feedback on train state, even when operating several trains at once

Panels DCC works equally well for hands-on driving and supervised operation, making it suitable for solo layouts, club layouts, and exhibition use.

Accessory Operation

Points, signals, and layout features — all in one place

Beyond train control, Panels DCC gives you centralised control of layout accessories.

Turnouts, signals, lighting, and other accessories are presented as first-class elements of the system, not afterthoughts. Each accessory can be controlled directly, grouped logically, or linked into routes and interlocking logic.

This allows you to:

  • Throw points and set signals with confidence
  • See the current state of accessories at a glance
  • Reduce operational errors during busy running sessions
  • Build layouts that behave consistently and predictably

Accessory control is designed to scale — from a small shunting plank to a complex multi-station layout.

Panel-Based Control

Operate your layout like a real signal box

One of the defining features of Panels DCC is its support for graphical control panels inspired by real railway signalling diagrams.

Instead of thinking in terms of addresses and commands, you operate the railway visually:

  • Tracks are shown as lines
  • Points and signals appear where they exist on the layout
  • Routes are set by interacting with the panel, not by operating each component individually

This approach makes operation more realistic, more intuitive, and far more engaging — especially when running complex movements or timetabled sessions.

Panel-based control also supports:

  • Route setting with automatic accessory changes
  • Clear indication of route status and conflicts
  • A shared visual reference for multiple operators
  • Train operation overlays for driving trains without changing screen

Designed for How You Run

Simple when it needs to be, powerful when it matters

Panels DCC is built around the idea that different layouts — and different operators — need different levels of control.

You can:

  • Drive trains directly
  • Operate accessories manually
  • Run entirely from a panel
  • Or mix all three approaches together

The system grows with your layout, allowing you to start simple and introduce more advanced operation over time — without having to rebuild everything from scratch.

A Foundation for Realistic Operation

At its core, Panels DCC is about making layout operation clearer, calmer, and more enjoyable. By unifying train control, accessory control, and panel-based operation, it supports layouts that feel closer to the real railway — without losing the fun of hands-on modelling.

Flexible Operating Patterns

One system, many ways to operate

The cloud-hosted, multi-device architecture enables flexible operating patterns that adapt to your layout and operating style:

  • A large screen or TV showing the overall panel and layout state
  • Tablets or phones used to drive individual trains
  • Devices dedicated to specific areas, yards, or stations
  • Multiple operators working together at the same time
  • Operators bringing their own devices to club layouts and exhibitions

Because configuration lives in the cloud, layouts can be designed, refined, and extended from anywhere — without needing to be physically connected to the railway.
